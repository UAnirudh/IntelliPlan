FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students need to know about IntelliPlan, from how it connects to Canvas and StudentVue, to how it builds your schedule and what it costs.

Is there a free alternative to Turbo AI or TurboLearn? ▾

Yes. IntelliPlan is a free alternative to Turbo AI for students. Like Turbo AI, it turns recorded lectures, audio files, PDFs, pasted text, and YouTube videos into notes, flashcards, and quizzes. Unlike note-only apps, it also imports assignments from Canvas, StudentVue, and Schoology, prioritizes them, builds a weekly study schedule, tracks grades, and includes the Plani AI tutor, all free.

Can IntelliPlan turn a recorded lecture into notes and flashcards? ▾

Yes. Record a lecture directly in IntelliPlan or upload an audio file (mp3, m4a, wav, webm, or ogg) and it transcribes the audio with AI, then generates notes, flashcards, and a quiz you can study right away.

Can IntelliPlan make flashcards and a quiz from a YouTube video? ▾

Yes. Paste a YouTube link and IntelliPlan pulls the video's transcript, then generates notes, flashcards, and a quiz from it. You can also paste a transcript or upload a PDF if a video has no captions available.

Can I use IntelliPlan to prioritize assignments for multiple classes at the same time? ▾

Yes. IntelliPlan pulls all assignments from every connected class, whether from Canvas, StudentVue, or Schoology, and ranks them across all subjects simultaneously using due date, point value, and estimated difficulty. You see one unified priority list, not one per class.

How does IntelliPlan connect to school tools to import assignments automatically? ▾

IntelliPlan integrates directly with Canvas LMS, StudentVue, and Schoology. After you connect your account, IntelliPlan pulls your assignments automatically. You don't have to copy anything manually. When your teacher updates a due date, IntelliPlan updates too.

What are the benefits of using an AI study plan generator like IntelliPlan? ▾

The main benefit is eliminating the time students waste deciding what to work on. IntelliPlan figures out what's most urgent based on your real deadlines, estimates how long each task will take, and builds a schedule around your actual free time. Students report spending less time planning and more time actually studying. It also reduces the anxiety of not knowing whether you're going to finish everything on time.

Can IntelliPlan help me reduce the time I spend figuring out what to do? ▾

That is exactly what IntelliPlan is built for. The app's core goal is to answer "what should I work on right now?" automatically. Instead of staring at five different portals trying to figure out what's due when, IntelliPlan gives you a ranked list and a schedule. Most users say their planning time drops from 20–30 minutes per day to under 5.

How much does IntelliPlan cost for students? ▾

IntelliPlan is free to get started, no credit card required. See the pricing page for current plan details.

Can I customize the study schedule that IntelliPlan generates for me? ▾

Yes. After IntelliPlan generates your schedule, you can adjust time estimates, change priorities, move sessions around, and block off times you're not available. The AI adapts to your edits - if you push something back, it recalculates the rest of your week automatically.

Is IntelliPlan compatible with Canvas and StudentVue? ▾

Yes. IntelliPlan supports Canvas LMS and StudentVue natively. It also supports Schoology and exports to Google Calendar and Notion. If your school uses Canvas or StudentVue, you can connect IntelliPlan in a few minutes and see all your assignments ranked by priority immediately.

Does IntelliPlan work for high school students? ▾

Yes. IntelliPlan works for both high school and college students. High school students often use StudentVue, which IntelliPlan connects to directly, something most competing planners don't support.

How is IntelliPlan different from MyStudyLife or Shovel? ▾

IntelliPlan connects to StudentVue, which neither MyStudyLife nor Shovel supports. IntelliPlan also includes a built-in Grade Modeler (simulate your final grade before you take a test), AI-generated schedules, and a Study & Learn tool for flashcards, all in one app. Shovel requires a paid subscription to access its scheduling features; IntelliPlan is free to start. See the full comparison here.

Does IntelliPlan replace my school portal like Canvas or StudentVue? ▾

No. IntelliPlan reads your assignments from those portals and organizes them. You still submit work through Canvas or StudentVue. IntelliPlan just tells you what to do and when to do it.

What happens if my teacher changes a due date? ▾

IntelliPlan re-syncs with your school tools automatically. When a due date changes in Canvas or StudentVue, your IntelliPlan priority list and schedule update to reflect it.

Can I use IntelliPlan on my phone? ▾

Yes. IntelliPlan is a Progressive Web App (PWA) available on iPhone and Android. You can install it from the website, no App Store required for Android, and it installs like a native app on iPhone via Safari. Visit the install page for step-by-step instructions.
