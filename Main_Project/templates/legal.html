Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect
When you sign in with Google, IntelliPlan receives your name and email address from Google. IntelliPlan does not store your Google password. Authentication is handled entirely by Google's secure OAuth system. For optional school integrations, IntelliPlan may store encrypted access credentials or tokens for services such as Canvas and StudentVue solely to fetch your assignments and related academic information. We also collect task completion data that you voluntarily provide to improve scheduling predictions and application functionality.
How we use data
IntelliPlan uses collected information only to provide and improve the features you request, including authentication, assignment synchronization, calendar integrations, and AI-generated scheduling assistance. We do not use personal data for advertising, resale, or unrelated profiling.
Data protection & security
We use industry-standard security measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Data transmitted between users and IntelliPlan is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Sensitive credentials and tokens are encrypted before storage. Access to stored data is restricted to authorized systems and personnel only. We regularly review our infrastructure and security practices to help maintain the confidentiality and integrity of user data.
What we do not collect
We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to provide the features you explicitly enable. We do not display advertising. We do not use tracking or analytics technologies. We do not store your assignment content beyond what is needed to display it to you.
Google Workspace API limited use disclosure
The use of raw or derived user data received from Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Third-party services
IntelliPlan uses Groq for AI scheduling, Google OAuth for sign-in, Google Calendar with your explicit permission, and Notion with your explicit permission. Each service operates under its own privacy policy. No social media platforms are integrated into IntelliPlan.
Data retention
User data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide IntelliPlan services and features. You may delete your account at any time from Settings.
Data deletion
You can delete your account at any time from Settings. All associated data is permanently deleted immediately. Parents or guardians may request deletion of a child's account by contacting us at the email below.
Students under 13 (COPPA)
IntelliPlan is open to students of all ages, including those under 13. We collect only the information reasonably necessary for the app to function. We do not use personal information for advertising or share it with third parties except as necessary to provide requested features. Parents or guardians may request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting us at the email below.
