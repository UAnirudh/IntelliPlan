Accessibility

Tune IntelliPlan for how you read and move best. Changes apply instantly and follow you to every device you sign in on.

Reading

Switches the interface to OpenDyslexic, designed with weighted bottoms to reduce letter-flipping.
Default is 100%. Larger sizes scale the whole interface.
Extra space between lines makes long passages easier to track.
Adds underlines to all in-content links so they don't rely on color alone.

Vision & focus

Boosts contrast across all surfaces to meet WCAG AAA. Removes faint borders and translucent backgrounds.
Thicker, higher-contrast outline when you tab between elements. Useful for keyboard navigation.

Motion

Disables animations and transitions across the app. Your OS-level "reduce motion" setting is also respected automatically.
