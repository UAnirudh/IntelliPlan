Accessibility

Tune IntelliPlan for how you read and move best. Changes apply instantly and follow you to every device you sign in on.

Reading
Dyslexia-friendly font Switches the interface to OpenDyslexic — weighted letter bottoms make characters harder to flip or mirror.
Text size Default is 100%. Larger sizes scale the whole interface, not just body text.
Line spacing Extra space between lines makes long passages easier to track with your eye.
Underline links Adds underlines to all in-content links so they don't rely on color alone.
Vision & focus
High contrast Boosts contrast across all surfaces to meet WCAG AAA. Removes faint borders and translucent backgrounds.
Bold focus indicators Thicker, higher-contrast outline when you tab between elements. Useful for keyboard navigation.
Motion
Reduce motion Disables animations and transitions across the app. Your OS-level "reduce motion" setting is also respected automatically.
