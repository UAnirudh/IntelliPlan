The short version

  • We use cookies to keep you signed in. Those cannot be turned off, because without them there is no sign-in.
  • Anything that is not strictly necessary is off until you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • We run no advertising cookies, no retargeting pixels, and no cross-site tracking. We do not sell data.
  • If you are under 13, analytics never runs, regardless of what any banner was clicked.
