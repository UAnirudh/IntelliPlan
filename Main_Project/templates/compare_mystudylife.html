IntelliPlan vs MyStudyLife

Both MyStudyLife and IntelliPlan help students plan their week with AI. The practical differences are which school connections you can use, how the planner fits your schedule, and what it helps you do once you start studying.

The core difference

MyStudyLife combines rotating school timetables, homework, and exam reminders. Scout, its AI study partner, can lay out your week or draft a revision plan around an exam.

IntelliPlan connects directly to Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVue, and Schoology, imports available assignments, ranks them by deadline and effort, and builds a weekly study schedule.

When MyStudyLife is the better pick

Try MyStudyLife if your rotating timetable is the main thing you need to manage, or if you want exam countdowns and revision tasks in one calendar.

When IntelliPlan is the better pick

Try IntelliPlan if your school uses StudentVue or Schoology, or if you want to follow a study block with step-by-step tutoring or practice questions from your notes. Check which connections your school allows before you switch.
