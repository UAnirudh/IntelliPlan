IntelliPlan vs MyStudyLife

MyStudyLife is a free, clean manual planner that students have used for years to track their class schedule and homework. IntelliPlan takes a different approach: instead of typing everything in, it imports your real assignments from your school's LMS and plans your week with AI.

The core difference

MyStudyLife is a manual planner. You enter your timetable, your classes, and your homework, and it shows them back to you in a clean dashboard. It's simple, free, and works across devices.

IntelliPlan connects directly to Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVue, and Schoology, pulls every assignment automatically, ranks them by deadline and effort, and builds a weekly study schedule.

When MyStudyLife is the better pick

If your school doesn't use a supported LMS (or you genuinely prefer typing your own assignments in), MyStudyLife is clean, free, and reliable. It's good at what it does — a digital paper planner.

When IntelliPlan is the better pick

If your school uses Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVue, Schoology, Blackboard, or Moodle, IntelliPlan saves you the manual entry and adds AI scheduling, grade tracking, and an AI tutor — all free. It's a planner that updates itself when teachers change due dates, which is the whole reason students stop using manual planners by week three.
