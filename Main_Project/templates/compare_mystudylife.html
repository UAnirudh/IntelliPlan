IntelliPlan vs MyStudyLife

MyStudyLife helps you manage a rotating school timetable and plan revision with Scout. IntelliPlan imports school assignments, estimates the work ahead, and schedules study sessions around your availability.

The core difference

MyStudyLife is built around a school timetable. It supports rotating and block schedules, and its Scout coach can create a timetable from a photo and plan revision around exams. MyStudyLife also lists course timetable sync from Canvas and Blackboard. MyStudyLife feature guide

IntelliPlan connects directly to Canvas, Google Classroom, StudentVUE, and Schoology, imports available assignments, ranks them by deadline and effort, and builds a weekly study schedule.

When MyStudyLife is the better pick

Choose MyStudyLife if your biggest concern is keeping classes straight across rotating days or alternating weeks. Scout also gives you an exam revision plan based on the dates you enter.

When IntelliPlan is the better pick

Choose IntelliPlan if your first question is what to start after school. It brings in assignments and deadlines, fits study blocks into your available time, and shows how much study time is still unallocated each day. The grade modeler helps you decide which assignments deserve more attention.
