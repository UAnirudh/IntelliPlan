1. Information we collect

To deliver a useful study planner we collect only what we need:

  • Account information — email address and password hash, or a Google identifier if you sign in with Google. Optionally a display name.
  • Academic data you provide or sync — assignment titles, due dates, class names, grades, test scores, and notes you save.
  • Integration tokens — OAuth tokens for Google Calendar, Notion, Canvas, Schoology, and StudentVUE so we can read/write the resources you connect. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest and used only to perform the actions you initiate.
  • Optional reminders data — phone number and push-notification subscription, only if you opt in. You can revoke either at any time.
  • Usage telemetry — anonymous error logs (Sentry) and basic event counts (e.g. "schedule generated"). We do not record screen video, keystrokes, or use session-replay tools.

2. How we use information

We use the data above strictly to:

  • Show your assignments and generate study schedules.
  • Sync the calendars, task lists, and gradebooks you ask us to.
  • Send reminders by SMS or browser push, only when you opt in.
  • Diagnose bugs and improve reliability.

We do not use student data to train advertising models or to target ads. We do not run ad networks, retargeting pixels, or behavioral profiling.

3. Data sharing & disclosure

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Services you connect — when you link Google Calendar, Notion, or an LMS, IntelliPlan acts as a client of those services on your behalf, using only the scopes you authorize.
  • Service providers — Railway (hosting), Twilio (SMS delivery), Groq (model inference), and Stripe (subscriptions, if you upgrade). Each is contractually bound to use data only to provide that service.
  • Legal compliance — when required by law, valid legal process, or to prevent fraud or imminent harm.
  • Schools (FERPA) — see Section 5.

We will never share or transfer student data to a third party for marketing, advertising, or commercial-profiling purposes.

4. Children under 13 (COPPA)

IntelliPlan complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • If you are under 13, we require verifiable parental or guardian consent before you can create an account. During sign-up you will be asked for your birth date. If it indicates you are under 13, the account is blocked until a parent's email address is provided and the parent confirms by clicking a verification link.
  • For students under 13 accessing IntelliPlan through a school, the school may act as the parent's agent for COPPA notice and consent, as permitted by the FTC's COPPA guidance. We rely on the school's permission in that case.
  • We collect only the minimum information needed and we do not condition participation on disclosure of more than is reasonably necessary.
  • Parents may review the personal information collected from their child, have it deleted, or refuse to allow further collection. Contact us using the address in Section 11.

5. FERPA & school-supplied data

When IntelliPlan is used in a school context, data you import from an LMS (Canvas, Schoology, StudentVUE) may include records covered by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). IntelliPlan acts as a "school official" under FERPA's school-official exception:

  • We use educational records only to provide the planning and study tools the student requested.
  • We do not redisclose those records to anyone except the student or the school district that authorized access.
  • We honor district or institutional requests to delete student records promptly.

6. Data security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; OAuth tokens are stored in an encrypted database column.
  • Access to production databases is restricted to a small number of maintainers and is logged.
  • No system is completely secure — we follow industry best practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-party integrations

You decide which services to connect. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google (Calendar, sign-in) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Notion — notion.so/Privacy-Policy
  • Canvas (Instructure) — instructure.com/policies/privacy
  • Twilio (SMS) — twilio.com/legal/privacy
  • Stripe (subscriptions) — stripe.com/privacy

8. Your rights & controls

  • Export your data — Settings → Export Data downloads a CSV of your assignments and tasks.
  • Disconnect integrations — Settings → Integrations.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete Account. All associated records are removed within 30 days.
  • Opt out of reminders — reply STOP to any SMS, or use Settings → Phone & reminders to clear both opt-ins.
  • Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you by emailing the address in Section 11.

9. Data retention & deletion

We retain personal data only as long as you have an active account, plus a short grace period for backups. Deleting your account removes your personal profile, assignments, tasks, and OAuth tokens. Aggregated, de-identified usage statistics may be retained.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. Material changes will be highlighted on the sign-in page and emailed to active users.
