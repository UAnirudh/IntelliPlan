Delete your IntelliPlan account

You can delete your IntelliPlan account yourself, at any time, from either the mobile app or the website. You do not need to contact us first, and you do not need to give a reason.

Deletion is permanent. There is no undo and no recovery window. If you only want to stop the emails or reminders, you can turn those off in Settings without deleting anything.

From the app

  1. Open IntelliPlan on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked. You will be signed out immediately.

From the website

  1. Sign in at intelliplan.tech.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

If you cannot sign in

Email {{ support_email }} from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We verify ownership of the address before acting, and we complete verified requests within 30 days — usually within a few business days.

Schools and parents may request deletion of a student's records on that student's behalf using the same address. We respond to those within 10 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes, immediately and permanently:

  • Your profile — name, email address, password, and account settings.
  • Every connected integration and its access tokens — Canvas, StudentVue, Schoology, Google Classroom, Google Calendar, Blackboard, Moodle, and Notion. Disconnecting also revokes IntelliPlan's access on the provider's side.
  • Your tasks, assignments, saved schedules, and day archives.
  • Your grades, imported or synced, and any predictions built from them.
  • Your study sessions, focus check-in summaries, streaks, points, and mastery history.
  • Your tutor conversations, lessons, notes, and uploaded files.
  • Your notifications, reminder preferences, push registrations, and API keys.
  • Your membership in any study group, and any tasks you created in one.

What is kept, and why

  • Study groups you created are not deleted — they belong to the other students in them too. The group stays, without an owner, and your posts and membership are removed.
  • Your email address on our unsubscribe list, if you ever unsubscribed. It is kept precisely so that unsubscribing outlives the account: without it, re-registering the same address would start the emails again.
  • Aggregated, de-identified usage statistics. These carry no identifiers and cannot be linked back to you or to any student.
  • Encrypted backups age out on their own rolling schedule and are fully expired within 90 days. They are never used to restore a deleted account.

Full detail is in our privacy policy. Questions about any of this go to {{ support_email }}.
