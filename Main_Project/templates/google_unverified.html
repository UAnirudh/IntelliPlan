Google will show you a warning

It's expected. Here's what it means before you see it.

On the next screen Google will say “Google hasn't verified this app”. That is Google's standard notice for any app requesting calendar access that has not yet completed their review. It is a statement about our review status, not about anything Google found wrong.

To continue past it, choose Advanced, then Go to IntelliPlan (unsafe). If you would rather not, that is completely reasonable — everything below still works without it.

What IntelliPlan does with your calendar
  • Reads when you are busy, so study blocks are not planned over your existing events.
  • Adds study blocks you explicitly choose to push, and nothing else.
  • Never reads event contents for any other purpose, and never shares them.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings, or revoke access directly from your Google account permissions. Full detail is in our privacy policy.
