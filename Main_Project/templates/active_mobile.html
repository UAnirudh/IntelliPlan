    Focus check-in — optional

    Nudges you if you drift off. Everything happens on this device.

    • Turn it on, then tap Start. Your phone asks for the camera once.
    • Checks every couple of seconds whether you're still there.
    • Nudges only after you've clearly gone — not for glancing away.
    • Switch it off and the camera stops immediately.

    Nothing that could identify you. Video is never recorded or uploaded — frames are checked on your phone and discarded. All we receive is a count per minute: how many checks saw someone, how many didn't. No images, no face data.
