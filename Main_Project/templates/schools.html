What students actually get

Automatic assignment import

Students connect their LMS once. Every assignment, due date, and grade-weight flows in automatically and updates as teachers change things.

AI priority + scheduling

Assignments are ranked by deadline, point value, and effort across every class — one unified list. A weekly study schedule is generated around each student's free time.

Plani Tutor

A built-in AI tutor that explains math, science, history, English, coding, languages, and test prep step by step. Always free for students.

Grade Modeler

"What do I need on my final to keep an A?" — IntelliPlan answers it with the student's real category weights and current scores, automatically.

Frequently asked

Does this cost the school anything?

No. IntelliPlan is completely free for every student at no cost to the school or district.

What student data does IntelliPlan access?

Only the assignment metadata the student explicitly authorizes through their LMS (titles, due dates, point values, course names). We don't read submissions, grades that haven't posted, or messages. Read-only access — IntelliPlan can't modify the LMS account. Full policy at /legal.

Do we need IT to set anything up?

No. Students authenticate through their existing LMS account, just like signing into any other service. No SSO config, no admin approvals, no rostering imports.

Is IntelliPlan COPPA compliant for K-12?

Yes — we comply with COPPA. Students under 13 require parental approval at signup. No student data is sold, ever.
