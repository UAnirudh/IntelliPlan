Camera source

USB webcams, capture cards and virtual cameras appear after your browser grants access. Pair another phone over the same Wi-Fi, or use a network camera exposed as a webcam. Selecting a source keeps the camera off until you enable the check-in.

Sign into the same account on both devices. The link expires in two minutes. Tap Connect on your phone to send video to this study browser. Audio is off; no video is recorded or sent to IntelliPlan. Keep both pages open on the same Wi-Fi. Turn check-ins off to disconnect.
