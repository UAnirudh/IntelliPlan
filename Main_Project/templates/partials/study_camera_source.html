Camera source

USB webcams, capture cards and virtual cameras appear after your browser grants access. Pair another phone over the same Wi-Fi, or connect an HTTPS network-camera gateway. Selecting a source keeps the camera off until you enable the check-in.

Sign into the same account on both devices. The link expires in two minutes. Tap Connect on your phone to send video to this study browser. Audio is off; no video is recorded or sent to IntelliPlan. Keep both pages open on the same Wi-Fi. Turn check-ins off to disconnect.

Use the camera gateway’s credentials. They are sent only to the gateway you enter, and are not stored or sent to IntelliPlan. Video comes directly from that gateway; IntelliPlan does not proxy or record it. RTSP and ONVIF cameras need a local WebRTC gateway with trusted HTTPS and website access enabled (CORS). The deployment must allow its origin. Enable the check-in after entering its address.
