Make time for your Schoology assignments

Use assignment due dates from Schoology to make a study plan that fits around class, practice, and work. You can try the planner with sample assignments before connecting anything.

Try the sample planner See school connection options

Connect when your school allows it

  1. Confirm with your school that it permits the connection and gives your account access to Schoology API credentials.
  2. Sign in to IntelliPlan, open the school connection screen, and choose Schoology.
  3. Check the imported assignments and deadlines before building a plan. The items available depend on what the school publishes through the connection.

Submit your work in Schoology. You can remove the connection in IntelliPlan or revoke your API key in Schoology. PowerSchool’s API key documentation.

Start with your own list

If your school limits API access, sign in to paste a list of assignments and review it before adding tasks. You can also paste a class timetable or scan your schedule to show the planner when you are busy.
