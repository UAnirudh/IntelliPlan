Can I plan without connecting a school portal?

Yes. Start with the sample planner or sign in to paste your tasks. You can also paste or scan your class schedule.

Can it work around practice or a shift?

Yes. Set your own study hours or give IntelliPlan calendar access so it can use busy time when scheduling your work.

What if a teacher moves a deadline?

IntelliPlan can update your plan after a supported connection refreshes. You can also rebuild it yourself from the scheduler.

How does IntelliPlan compare with Matcha?

Both help plan study time around classes and commitments. See how they differ on planning, tutoring, and grade scenarios.
