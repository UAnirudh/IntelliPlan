How to calculate your weighted grade

Multiply each category's average by its weight, add the results, and divide by the total weight. Example: Homework (20%, 95% avg) and Tests (80%, 85% avg) give an 87% overall. If your weights don't total 100% yet, this calculator normalizes by the total so far, handy mid-semester.
