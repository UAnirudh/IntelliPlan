Common questions

Is the free plan actually free — what's the catch?

Yes, genuinely free. Connect Canvas, StudentVue, or Schoology, get AI priority scoring, schedule up to 2 study plans per day, and chat with Plani up to 50 times a month — no credit card ever required.

What happens when I hit the free limits?

You'll see a prompt explaining what you hit and what Pro unlocks. You can dismiss it and keep using everything else for free. We don't lock you out of the app — just the specific over-limit feature.

Can I cancel Pro at any time?

Yes. Cancel from your Lemon Squeezy subscription portal anytime. You'll keep Pro access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

How does the AI scheduler limit work for free users?

Free users can generate 2 AI study schedules every 24 hours. The counter resets daily. Pro users have no limit.

How does IntelliPlan compare to paid tools like Shovel?

Shovel charges a yearly or lifetime subscription for its core scheduling. IntelliPlan's free tier already includes AI scheduling, StudentVue support (which Shovel lacks), and Grade Modeling. Pro adds unlimited AI usage and advanced features at $4.99/month. See the full comparison →
