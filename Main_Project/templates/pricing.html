How do I cancel Pro?

From Your plan, or from the link in the Stripe receipt. Pro stays on until the end of the month or year already paid for, then the account goes back to the free plan with everything intact.

Your plan page gives you a link to send to a parent or guardian. They pay on Stripe without creating an account, and they see only your first name.
