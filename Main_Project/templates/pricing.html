Pricing questions

Is IntelliPlan actually free? What's the catch?

Yes, it's genuinely free to use. There's no hidden paywall or trial period — you can connect Canvas, StudentVue, or Schoology and use every feature without paying anything. If we introduce a paid tier in the future, we'll announce it well in advance and existing features will stay accessible.

Do I need to enter a credit card to sign up?

No. You can create an account and connect your school tools with just an email address (or Google sign-in). No credit card is ever required to get started.

How does IntelliPlan compare to paid tools like Shovel?

Shovel charges a yearly or lifetime subscription to access its core scheduling features. IntelliPlan gives you AI scheduling, LMS integration (including StudentVue, which Shovel doesn't support), and Grade Modeling for free. See the full comparison here.
