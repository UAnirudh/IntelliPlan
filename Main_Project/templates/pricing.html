What a free plan usually means

And what it does not mean here.

  • Nothing is held back for a paid tier. There is no paid tier. AI scheduling, grade modeling and the tutor are the product, not the upsell.
  • No trial clock. Accounts do not expire and features do not switch off after two weeks.
  • Your school data stays yours. IntelliPlan reads only what you authorize through official OAuth or a scoped login, and you can wipe it from Settings.
  • Sign-in takes one field. Email or Google, then straight into the planner. No card, no sales call.
  • It runs where you already are. Desktop, phone, and installable as a Progressive Web App on iOS, Android and Chromebook.

Common questions

Is IntelliPlan really free forever?

Yes. IntelliPlan is free forever with no sign-up barriers. You can connect Canvas, StudentVue, or Schoology, build AI study schedules, model your grades, and use the built-in study tools without any feature gates.

How does IntelliPlan stay free?

IntelliPlan is built for students first. We keep the core planning experience fully accessible so every student can benefit from assignment prioritization, scheduling, and tutoring without friction.

Can I get started right away?

Yes. You can create an account and connect your school tools with just an email address or Google sign-in. IntelliPlan works immediately after sign-up.

Are there any limits on the free plan?

IntelliPlan gives students access to scheduling, grade modeling, assignment prioritization, and AI study tools. The experience is designed to support students without hiding key features behind separate account levels.

How does IntelliPlan compare to other planners?

IntelliPlan includes LMS integration, AI scheduling, grade modeling, and study tools in one experience. Many other planners are either manual, require more setup, or do not support direct Canvas/StudentVue integration.
