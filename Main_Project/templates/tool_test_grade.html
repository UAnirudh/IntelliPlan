How to calculate a test grade

Divide the number of correct answers by the total number of questions, then multiply by 100. For example, 18 correct out of 20 questions is 18 ÷ 20 = 0.9 = 90%, an A−/B on a standard scale (90–100 A, 80–89 B, 70–79 C, 60–69 D, below 60 F).
