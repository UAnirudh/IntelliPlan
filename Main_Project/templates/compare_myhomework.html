IntelliPlan vs myHomework

myHomework is a long-running, well-designed homework tracker. IntelliPlan goes a step further: instead of you typing in assignments, it imports them from your school account and plans your week with AI.

The core difference

myHomework is a clean digital planner where you add classes and homework manually and check them off. It works across devices and is familiar to a lot of students. IntelliPlan connects directly to Canvas, StudentVue, and Schoology, so your assignments appear automatically, then it prioritizes them and builds a study schedule for you.

When myHomework is the better pick

If you prefer entering work by hand and want a simple, no-frills tracker that has been around for years, myHomework does that well and many students like its simplicity.

When IntelliPlan is the better pick

If your school uses Canvas, StudentVue, or Schoology and you would rather not retype every assignment, IntelliPlan saves that effort and adds AI prioritization, scheduling, grade modeling, and an AI tutor, all free. It is the difference between tracking your homework and having a plan for it.
