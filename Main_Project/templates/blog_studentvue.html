Does Any Study Planner Connect to StudentVue? Yes, Here's How

If you're a high school student, there's a good chance your school uses StudentVue to track grades, attendance, and assignments. And if you've ever tried to find a study planner that connects to it, you've probably noticed the answer is almost always no.

Apps like Shovel and MyStudyLife support Canvas or manual entry, but they skip StudentVue entirely. IntelliPlan is different: it's the only student study planner with native StudentVue integration.

Why StudentVue support matters

Thousands of high schools across the United States use Synergy StudentVue as their student information system. It's where your assignments, grades, and attendance live. But it's not designed to help you plan your work, it just displays it.

Without a planner that connects to StudentVue, your options are to manually copy every assignment into a separate app (tedious and error-prone) or try to plan from StudentVue directly (not what it's built for). IntelliPlan closes that gap.

How to connect IntelliPlan to StudentVue

  1. Go to intelliplan.tech and create a free account.
  2. During onboarding, select StudentVue as your school system.
  3. Enter your district's StudentVue URL, your username, and your password.
  4. IntelliPlan will pull in your current assignments and grades automatically.
  5. Within seconds you'll see a prioritized list, High, Medium, Low, across every subject.

IntelliPlan doesn't store your StudentVue credentials after connecting. Your data stays between you and your district's portal.

What you get after connecting

After connecting StudentVue, IntelliPlan builds a ranked task list from all your assignments. Each item is scored by urgency (due date), importance (point value), and estimated effort. You get one clean list instead of navigating class by class through StudentVue.

You can also generate a full AI study schedule for the week and export it to Google Calendar, so your study plan actually shows up where you plan your time. The Grade Modeler lets you simulate how different scores on upcoming assignments will affect your final grade.

The only planner built for StudentVue users

If you've been looking for a study planner that works with StudentVue and haven't found one, IntelliPlan is it. It's free to get started, takes about 3 minutes to connect, and works for both high school and college students.
