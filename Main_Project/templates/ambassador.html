How it works

  1. Create your account at intelliplan.tech and grab your unique referral link from the ambassador dashboard. It looks like intelliplan.tech/ref/your-code.
  2. Share it with students at your school. Drop it in your school Discord, GroupMe, Reddit, class chats, or your study-content TikTok bio — wherever students actually hang out.
  3. Track your referrals in real time from your dashboard. We show you who signed up, your tier, and how close you are to the next reward.
  4. Get perks. Gift cards and swag ship at the end of each month for ambassadors who hit the thresholds.

Frequently asked

Who can become an ambassador?

Any current high school or college student in good standing. You don't need a big social following — most ambassadors grow through their class chats, study groups, and school Discord servers.

Does it cost anything?

No. The program is free to join. We give you rewards because student-to-student recommendations are the most effective way to grow.

What counts as an "active referral"?

A signup that comes through your unique link and connects at least one LMS (Canvas, StudentVue, Schoology, or Google Classroom) or generates a study schedule in their first 7 days.

When do gift cards ship?

End of each calendar month, by email to the address on your IntelliPlan account, within 5 business days of month-end.

Can I represent more than one school?

Yes — but most ambassadors focus on one campus. We'll feature you in multi-school spotlights if you grow IntelliPlan at more than one institution.
