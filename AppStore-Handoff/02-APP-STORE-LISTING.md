## Description  *(max 4000 chars)*
```
IntelliPlan is the AI-powered study planner built by a student, for students. Connect your school account and turn a messy list of due dates into a clear, personalized plan — so you always know what to work on next.

Canvas and StudentVue show you WHAT'S due. IntelliPlan tells you WHEN to do it, WHAT to prioritize, and HOW LONG it'll take.

CONNECT YOUR SCHOOL
• Canvas LMS, StudentVue, and Schoology — assignments, due dates, and grades import automatically
• Link more than one account at once
• Add your own manual tasks too

AI STUDY SCHEDULER
• Tell it your available hours and when you study best
• Get a complete, multi-day study plan broken into focused blocks with breaks
• Export your plan straight to Google Calendar in one tap

SMART PRIORITIES
• Every assignment is scored High / Medium / Low
• Estimated time per task based on points, due date, and course weight
• A Notion-style board with Overdue, Today, and Upcoming

STUDY & LEARN
• Upload your notes (PDF, DOCX, TXT, and more) and instantly get flashcards, key concepts, summaries, and practice quizzes
• Built-in spaced repetition tracks what you've mastered and schedules reviews

PLANI — YOUR AI TUTOR
• A step-by-step tutor for math, science, history, English, CS, languages, economics, and test prep
• It builds understanding instead of just handing over answers

GRADES & GRADE MODELER
• See your GPA at a glance
• Simulate "what if I get X% on my next test?" and see exactly how it changes your grade

MORE
• Deadline reminders so nothing slips
• Google Calendar and Notion integrations
• Light and dark mode

IntelliPlan keeps everything — your assignments, schedule, notes, and tutor — in one place, so you can stop juggling apps and actually stay ahead.

Made by a student who got tired of falling behind. Now it's yours.

Questions or feedback? Visit https://intelliplan.tech
```

## Age Rating
Answer the questionnaire honestly. IntelliPlan should land at **4+** in most cases. Note:
because it includes an **AI tutor/chat** and lets users reach a web-connected service, if
the questionnaire asks about user-generated or unrestricted web content, answer truthfully
— it may bump to **9+/12+**. That's fine; just don't claim 4+ if a question genuinely
applies. See file 03 for more.
