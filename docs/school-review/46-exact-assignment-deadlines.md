# Exact assignment deadline times

Explicit assignment timestamps now survive Canvas collection, Scheduler and Plani chat handoffs, day allocation, clock placement, saved-plan reconstruction, autopilot deadline changes and next-action overrides. Aware timestamps convert to the student IANA timezone (fixed offset fallback); date-only assignments retain date-only behavior. A future morning deadline cannot use that evening's free hours. Finishing exactly at the deadline is allowed.

Allocation checks shared clock capacity, including breaks and competing assignments, before choosing a day. The same calendar-gap sizing routine is used for tentative feasibility and final rendering: new flexible work can split across short gaps, while pinned/existing sittings retain their sizes. Work that cannot fit remains an explicit deferral with its deadline and minutes. Final validation independently rejects work after a future deadline, even if a caller labels it overdue recovery. Already-past deadlines retain the existing explicitly reported recovery behavior.

Autopilot detects changes to the time alone and removal of an exact time. Schedule adjustments preserve deadline metadata through move, shorten and skip deferrals. Plani's actual tool path is exercised with a disposable opted-in Los Angeles account and a mocked Canvas assignment due Wednesday09:00 local; returned and persisted work finishes Tuesday evening. No provider request, production student or production consent was used.
