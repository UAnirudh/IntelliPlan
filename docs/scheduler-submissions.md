# Schedule work that still needs you

The Scheduler's **Assignments** section separates **Needs attention** from **Waiting for grade**. Confirmed individual submission evidence puts an assignment in the waiting list. A missing score, a class-wide submission count or “Not Graded” alone does not establish that you submitted it.

If the school feed has not caught up, choose **I've submitted this**. IntelliPlan labels this as your own report, saves it for your account and leaves the work out of new study plans. **Undo submitted** restores it to the actionable list. These actions do not submit work to your school, change its due date or alter the teacher's gradebook. A teacher's explicit request to redo or resubmit work takes precedence over an earlier submission report.

For work that still needs action:

1. Set the time you can actually study. An exact daily window is optional; your weekly availability and class commitments also apply.
2. Connect Google or Outlook if you want appointments included. **Auto** checks three days across the connected calendars. Full or unavailable calendars show an explanation and preserve your settings.
3. Generate the plan. Sessions have actual start and end times, breaks and a task to work on. Exam preparation can be spread across sittings. Submitted work is excluded; unfinished overdue work can receive recovery time without changing the original deadline.
4. Inspect any deferred work. If there is not enough usable time, the scheduler shows what did not fit; it cannot make an impossible deadline feasible.
5. Save the plan and explicitly export it to your calendar when ready. Repeating an unchanged export is protected against duplicate events. Partial calendar errors remain visible.

The assignment collector and scheduling adapters use the same submission evidence rules. Read-only school feeds may not expose individual submission state at all; in that case the explicit student report is available rather than guessing from an absent grade.
