Status: local implementation and policy corrections; badges published separately in PRs #75 and #76; **not a legal compliance certification or clearance for an international, school, or under-13 launch**. Operator brand: IntelliPlan. The owner says no LLC has been formed, and NSD/Lakeside permission is being sought. No signed school or provider agreement was supplied. Intended users include US and international students; destination countries remain unspecified.

## Delivered behavior

- Study Buddies is optional. The unchecked sharing control explains display name, whether each person studied today, timer focus minutes today, joint current/longest streak and streak risk, reminders, historical activity, and existing-pair resumption. It excludes grades, classes, assignments and email addresses from buddy views.
- Both students must explicitly acknowledge `buddies_v1` and enable sharing before a mutually confirmed connection displays activity. Requests and confirmations cannot silently enable sharing. Consent version/time are stored. Old enabled accounts without this evidence are paused until renewed consent. Disabling clears consent and pauses all pair views; removing/blocking stops that connection. Already queued reminders and information someone saved cannot be recalled.
- Existing student-role, conservative birth-year age, unknown-age exclusion, under-13 exclusion, and required-parental-approval eligibility checks remain in the buddy service. Birth-year checks intentionally use the youngest possible age; they are not identity verification or country-specific age assurance.
- Signup and OAuth age completion now use the same conservative age calculation. Signed-in accounts awaiting parental approval cannot submit notes/syllabuses or use ordinary product endpoints. Legal, age review, consent and deletion routes remain available.
- Parental links now open an explanatory review page. GET requests cannot approve or delete accounts, including email-scanner visits. Approval/removal requires an explicit POST selection; approval records notice version/time and consumes the token. Signup email and review page use one direct-notice source. **An inbox link and checkbox still do not independently verify an adult or establish compliant verifiable parental consent.**
- Signup records actual current policy acknowledgements. Existing accounts receive privacy v3 and terms v2 notices; creation date alone is no longer treated as acceptance of these versions. A policy acknowledgement is separate from optional buddy, analytics and marketing consent.
- Optional analytics discloses account/pseudonymous visitor identifiers rather than claiming anonymity. Unknown-age/under-13 users are excluded by the event collector. No third-party analytics script is added. Operational logs are a separate purpose.
- Gemini Developer API routing is removed. Gemini chat, vision, audio and embeddings require `GEMINI_MINORS_CONTRACT_APPROVED`, a project and region, and Vertex credentials. **Keep this flag unset unless a written agreement expressly permits this student-facing use. Standard Vertex terms also restrict services likely to be accessed by under-18s; buying Cloud access is insufficient.** Existing other-provider fallbacks remain; their contracts and safeguards are not certified by this change.
- Account deletion removes the exact stored note/lesson files for the account and preserves other accounts' files. Unsafe stored paths fail deletion. Existing-table SQL failures now roll back database deletion rather than silently skipping personal records. Optional absent feature tables are inspected before execution. Uploads are staged under unique deletion filenames and restored if SQL deletion fails. After successful database commit, staged files are removed. A final filesystem cleanup error reports incomplete erasure and logs the need for support cleanup; that case requires an operational retry process.
- Privacy, terms, cookies and public school copy now distinguish implemented controls from unverified legal/operational claims. School approval is conditional; no NSD/Lakeside endorsement, blanket school-official status, provider retention guarantee or accessibility certification is asserted.
- Accessibility contract checks identified existing missing lesson/group label associations and a missing initial sidebar expanded state. These are corrected; the shared sidebar script already maintains its state. Full WCAG conformance still requires manual and assistive-technology review.
- The landing page includes the supplied 1000 Saas, SaasHunt, DevHub and Boost Domain Rating links with new-tab isolation. To support provider verification, images now use the exact external URLs supplied by the owner; Boost Domain Rating updates from its live endpoint. These image providers receive normal request metadata such as visitor IP addresses. Images suppress the page referrer; no account, academic or buddy information is included in image URLs. This replaces the initially staged local snapshots.

## Owner decisions and launch blockers

1. **Identify the actual legal operator now.** IntelliPlan is a brand, not evidence of a legal entity. Choose the person/entity responsible, usable postal address, telephone and monitored privacy contact; establish contracting authority and an adult account holder where required. Formation can follow a business decision, but collecting data or selling plans already creates obligations. Do not list an invented LLC.
2. **Choose the initial countries and eligible ages.** “International” is not a single jurisdiction. Commission a country/state-specific review of lawful bases, consent ages, rights, contracts, accessibility and consumer requirements. EU consent-based child thresholds vary by Member State; a US under-13 rule is insufficient. UK child-facing service design needs its own assessment. Record DPIAs, cross-border safeguards and representation requirements where applicable. The implementation does not currently enforce these regional differences.
3. **Approve an actual parental-verification design before under-13 release.** Current email-only approval is insufficient evidence for the service's provider/group disclosures. Determine VPC method, direct-notice required operator details, separate disclosure permissions, authority checks, renewal/revocation, child data minimization and pending-account deletion schedule. Known pending accounts are restricted, but legacy/guest/extension users without age information are not comprehensively prevented from submitting learning data across the product. Family roles are not independently verified. Resolve these entry points before representing child compliance.
4. **Obtain written school permission and data terms before school deployments.** NSD/Lakeside remain pending. Decide consumer vs school-authorized processing, permitted features, school direction/control, recipient access, student rights, security/incident notice, retention/return/destruction, vendor approval and contract assignment. A district's general internet-use consent is not authorization for this vendor. The app does not yet have a per-school authorization gate.
5. **Approve every child-facing processor and AI route.** Obtain applicable terms/DPA, retention/training controls, transfer basis, permitted age range, regional configuration and safety requirements. Gemini standard Developer and Cloud terms are restrictive for this audience; obtain an express exception or use a provider that permits the intended audience. Groq/Anthropic/media/voice fallbacks still need review. Do not enable the Gemini exception flag on the assumption that Vertex alone solves this. Define moderation, age assurance, monitoring and incident escalation for minor-facing AI.
6. **Verify and operate retention/security commitments.** Establish a named owner, written security program, access inventory, encryption/key management, breach response, log redaction and tested rights workflow. Schedule and monitor event/pending-account/log purges; reconcile backups with published 90-day limit; confirm exports/deletions across providers/storage. Verify the 30-day support process and distinguish limited lawful retention from indefinite storage. This repository audit did not inspect live configurations or run production deletion.
7. **Review paid-plan and marketing contracts before monetization.** Specify operator, prices/taxes, renewal frequency, consent, cancellation/refund process and mandatory consumer protections for selected countries. Ensure legal/adult payment account authority, physical mailing address and real unsubscribe behavior. Formation, school approvals and payment-provider acceptance are separate decisions.
8. **Prepare evidence for a future acquisition.** Establish ownership/assignments for founder/contributor work, trademarks/domains, dependency and asset licenses, executed vendor/customer contracts, data map, security records, consent history and incident/rights logs. Review assignability and successor privacy restrictions. An acquisition is not permission to repurpose student data. Code and policy improvements do not guarantee a valuation or sale.

Until these decisions have evidence, use a restricted review/pilot scope agreed with qualified privacy/education counsel, and do not describe the service as globally compliant, COPPA-certified, school-approved, SOC 2 certified or accessibility-certified. The consent and policy changes remain local. The separate badge-only publication in PRs #75/#76 adds external public image requests; it does not deploy the consent, account, AI-provider or policy changes.
